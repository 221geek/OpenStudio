# Poster quality gate

Reject a proposed visual when any of these conditions are true:

- The design could describe any event without changing its structure.
- The title, date and action have no evident reading order at thumbnail size.
- The layout is centered by default without a reference-led reason.
- A circle, wave or colour block is the only visual idea.
- A brand hex value, font name, palette role or analysis note appears in public copy.
- A reference is supplied but its crop, density, hierarchy and rhythm are absent from the proposal.
- A placeholder image area is presented as an illustration or as a finished visual.

Approve only when the design has one dominant message, meaningful negative space, a specific compositional choice, preserved practical information and a clear action.

For event posters, ensure the deadline/date remains legible in one glance. For social formats, ensure the title retains hierarchy when the canvas is reduced to a thumbnail. For inspiration-led work, articulate which concrete parts of the reference are being carried forward before generating the final composition.
